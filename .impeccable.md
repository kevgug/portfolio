## Design Context

### Users

People evaluating Kevin Gugelmann's product, design, and engineering work, typically browsing a portfolio on desktop or mobile and looking for a clear sense of craft, range, and point of view.

### Brand Personality

Thoughtful, refined, and quietly confident. The interface should feel editorial and personal, with interaction details that make the site feel considered without becoming noisy.

### Aesthetic Direction

Dark, typography-led portfolio with warm off-white surfaces and glacial blue as the signature interaction accent. Keep the existing Euclid Square and SangBleu Sunrise pairing, favoring precise outlines, restrained motion, and clear hover/focus affordances.

### Design Principles

- Make every interactive element visibly responsive before and during interaction.
- Use glacial blue consistently as the active, navigational, and focus accent.
- Preserve the calm editorial hierarchy of the portfolio through restrained motion.
- Keep typography and spacing consistent across the homepage and essays experience.
- Treat keyboard focus and reduced-motion preferences as first-class interaction states.
